# VylexAI Closed Beta — Tester Onboarding Email

**Send to:** all 17 Genesis Beta Testers (and any newly added Gmail addresses)
**From:** hello@vylexai.com
**Important:** the recipient's Gmail must already be on the Genesis Beta Testers list in Play Console, otherwise the opt-in link will show "not available."

---

## Subject
You're in — activate your VylexAI beta access (2 minutes)

---

## Body (English)

Hi {first name},

Thank you for joining the VylexAI Genesis Closed Beta. The app is live and your access is ready — here's how to install it. It takes about 2 minutes.

**Step 1 — Become a tester**
On your Android phone (signed in with the Gmail address you gave us), open this link:
👉 https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.vylexai.app
Tap **"Become a tester"**.

**Step 2 — Install from Google Play**
On the same page, tap **"Download it on Google Play"** (or open the Play Store link below). It can take up to a couple of hours after opting in for the app to appear — if it says "not available" at first, wait a bit and retry.
👉 https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vylexai.app

**Step 3 — Run it**
Open VylexAI → choose **Provider** (lend compute, earn BSAI) → flip the switch to **Active**. Your phone starts contributing AI compute to the network. You can also explore **Client** mode to submit AI tasks.

**Important — please keep it installed for the full 14 days.** Google requires testers to stay opted in for 14 continuous days for the beta to count. Even if you only open it occasionally, please don't uninstall before then.

Found a bug or have feedback? Just reply to this email, or write us at **hello@vylexai.com**.

Thank you for being one of the first — you're helping prove that distributed AI compute on everyday phones actually works.

— The VylexAI Team
🌐 vylexai.com

## Notes for the sender (strip before sending)
- Replace `{first name}` per recipient, or send as a generic "Hi there".
- The opt-in link only works if the recipient's Gmail is on the tester list (Play Console → Test → Geschlossener Test → Tester → Genesis Beta Testers).
- Only Google accounts (Gmail / Google Workspace) work — Outlook/iCloud will not.
- Must be opened on the Android device signed into that Google account.
